01 — Inbox, assembled Scroll — the method

The method

It learns your hand, not a house style.

Most assistants write like a press release. Marlowe studies the mail you already sent — the greetings, the clauses, the way you say no — and drafts inside that voice.

01

Reads the whole thread

Every participant, every attachment, every promise made three weeks ago in a message you have since forgotten.

02

Drafts in your register

Sentence length, warmth, sign-off. Tuned on writing you own, never on a shared template library.

03

Sending stays yours

The draft waits in the thread, editable and attributed. The send key is still a human hand — yours.

The reply is ready before you are.
