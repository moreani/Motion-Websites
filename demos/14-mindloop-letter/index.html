01 — What you get Four things, nothing else

Longhand is a reading room, not a feed. Letters arrive whole, at a set hour, written by people who answer their own mail. You read at your pace, and the page ends.

    No pixels, no ad auctions, no recommendation engine. We count opens, then forget them.
