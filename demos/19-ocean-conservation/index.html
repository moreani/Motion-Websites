02 — Method

How the count is kept

No modelled estimates, no gap-filling. Two divers, eight frames, and a third opinion whenever the tallies disagree by more than four individuals.

  1. 01

    Fixed quadrats

    Eight one-metre frames are bolted to the substrate at each station, so every survey photographs the same 8 m², at the same angle, at the same tide.

  2. 02

    Counted twice, blind

    Two analysts score each frame independently, without seeing the other's sheet. The pair never work the same station twice in a year.

  3. 03

    Published raw

    Counts, frames and disagreement logs go to the open archive within 60 days. Nothing is smoothed before release, including the years that look bad.

Inter-observer agreement, 2026 — 94.2% “A missing year cannot be reconstructed. That is the entire argument for showing up.” Field lead: Dr I. Marchetti
