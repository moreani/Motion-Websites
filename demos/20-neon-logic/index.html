[ 01 ]  System

One loop,
three layers.

Capture happens on the array. Decoding happens on the device. Nothing leaves the headset unless the participant asks it to, which is the only version of this we would wear.

  1. L1Capture

    Array

    1,024 iridium microelectrodes on a 1.5 mm shank, inserted through a single 4 mm burr hole. No cortical surface is removed.

  2. L2Decode

    Intention

    An on-device model resolves spike trains into intention at 20 kHz, with 11.8 ms end to end and no round trip to a server.

  3. L3Return

    Feedback

    Stimulation closes the loop inside the same cycle, so the interface reads as a sense rather than a command.
